# Institutional Alpha Research Campaign Preregistration v1

## Purpose

This layer freezes the first institutional alpha-research campaign contracts before empirical campaign results are viewed. It is a research-governance and falsification framework, not a profitability claim and not a live-trading authorization.

The canonical machine-readable preregistrations are under `research/preregistrations/v1/`. Their SHA-256 content fingerprints are generated by `quant_system.research.campaigns` and are part of downstream experiment lineage.

## Campaign families

1. `ALPHA-FUNDING-BASIS-CARRY-001` — funding / basis / carry.
2. `ALPHA-ORDER-FLOW-MICRO-001` — order-flow / microstructure.
3. `ALPHA-LIQUIDITY-SHOCK-MR-001` — liquidity shock / mean reversion.
4. `ALPHA-MOMENTUM-TREND-001` — momentum / trend.
5. `ALPHA-VOLATILITY-REGIME-001` — volatility / regime.
6. `ALPHA-CROSS-SECTION-PERPS-001` — cross-sectional crypto perpetuals.

Each campaign freezes, before results are viewed: economic hypothesis, Feature Engine inputs, bounded parameter axes and search budgets, costs/slippage, funding/financing treatment, PIT universe rules, chronological train/validation/locked-OOS windows, purged/walk-forward design, minimum samples/trades, rejection conditions, sensitivity tests, bootstrap/Monte Carlo requirements, multiple-testing treatment, DSR/PBO/reality-check gates, liquidity/capacity constraints and promotion requirements.

## Common temporal design

All v1 campaigns use the same preregistered UTC windows to reduce family-specific period selection:

- Training: 2023-01-01 through 2025-06-30 23:59:59.
- Validation: 2025-07-01 through 2026-03-31 23:59:59.
- Locked OOS: 2026-04-01 through 2026-09-21 23:59:59.
- Rolling walk-forward: 180-day train / 30-day test / 30-day step, at least 6 completed folds.
- Purge: at least 48 hours for overlapping information/label windows.
- Embargo: 24 hours after each test span.

The locked OOS is single-use. Any tuning, feature-definition edit, universe edit or parameter-space edit after OOS opening invalidates the v1 campaign and requires a new version/fingerprint.

## Common statistical gate

Promotion to a research `CANDIDATE` requires all of the following, not a subset:

- OOS mean return strictly positive after transaction costs, slippage, impact, funding and financing.
- Walk-forward positive fraction >= 2/3.
- Parameter-robust profitable fraction >= 2/3.
- Deflated Sharpe probability >= 0.95 using total family trial count.
- CSCV probability of backtest overfitting <= 0.25.
- White-style reality-check p-value <= 0.05.
- Holm-Bonferroni family-wise correction at alpha 0.05.
- Circular-block bootstrap lower 95% mean bound > 0 with >= 5,000 simulations.
- Monte Carlo trade-path analysis with >= 5,000 simulations.
- Base, 1.5x and 2.0x cost stresses; 2.0x must remain net-positive.
- All minimum trade/sample, lineage and liquidity/capacity gates satisfied.

All attempted configurations, including abandoned/rejected experiments, count against the family trial ledger. Refactoring does not reset multiplicity.

## Cost, funding and capacity policy

The primary backtest uses PIT venue/tier fees when available; where unavailable, the preregistration imposes a research floor of 2 bps maker / 5 bps taker. Slippage is half-spread plus an adverse 2 bps floor and square-root participation impact. Family JSON files state primary order style; optimistic passive queue assumptions are not permitted in primary microstructure tests.

Funding cashflows are applied only when known at their PIT settlement/availability time. Missing required funding observations across a funding settlement fail closed instead of being treated as zero. Quote-cash financing is stressed at 0%, 5% and 10% ACT/365; promotion must survive 5%.

Primary simulated participation is capped at 1% of observed interval traded quote volume and, when L2 is required, 5% of reconstructed top-five book notional. Violations are not silently filled.

## F3 / F6 integration

`PreregisteredCampaign.statistical_gate.to_validation_thresholds()` maps campaign thresholds directly into the existing F3 `ValidationThresholds` / `assess_candidate` machinery.

`PreregisteredCampaign.to_hypothesis()` maps the immutable campaign into an F6 `Hypothesis` with a deterministic UUID derived from the campaign fingerprint and precommitted `AcceptanceCriteria`. `to_strategy_blueprint()` exposes the preregistered falsification suite through the existing F6 `StrategyBlueprint` contract. `research_budget()` maps the family caps into `ResearchTrialBudget`.

`assess_campaign_candidate()` layers the campaign-specific sample, bootstrap, Monte Carlo, multiplicity, capacity and lineage gates on top of F3 assessment. Synthetic tests validate this wiring only; they are explicitly not market-edge evidence.

## R1.3 / Feature lineage integration

`CampaignLineageBinding.bind()` is now the empirical binding boundary. It requires a complete A01-A28 Alpha-v1 data-binding report, independently validates that report, requires `ALPHA_DATA_BINDING_READY=true`, requires the exact campaign to be binding-ready, and then rechecks the concrete R1.3 and FeatureRun lineage before constructing a verified binding.

The internal `_bind_lineage_only()` helper exists only so lower-level lineage semantics can be unit-tested without fabricating REAL MARKET evidence. An unverified lineage object cannot create an `ExperimentManifest`.

For a verified binding:

- the R1.3 dataset format matches the preregistration;
- the binding report manifest/dataset/universe identities equal the concrete R1.3 manifest;
- the required Feature Engine version matches;
- every Feature run contains the R1.3 manifest fingerprint in its input dataset fingerprints;
- every Feature run carries the exact R1.3 universe fingerprint;
- every requested FeatureRun fingerprint is covered by the accepted binding report; and
- no feature decision time exceeds the dataset manifest decision time.

The verified binding fingerprint and resulting `ExperimentManifest` retain the A01-A28 binding ID and decision fingerprint alongside the R1.3 manifest, universe, campaign and feature-run lineage. This preserves dataset -> feature -> binding decision -> experiment identity without changing R1.2, F7 or broker submission boundaries.
